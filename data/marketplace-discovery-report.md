## Evidence and limits

Each ledger entry records its primary URL, supporting URLs, access, purpose, platform evidence, and retrieval mode. **Page** means a readable primary page was retrieved. **Indexed primary listing** means the search service returned the publisher's listing text, sometimes despite an empty or blocked direct open. Neither mode establishes that a download works or that the product is independently reviewed.

Gumroad sometimes returned zero-line direct pages. Superhive's BlenderEXR product request returned HTTP 429; its indexed product/documentation established the requirements. Blender to Resolve Bridge exceeded the direct-fetch content limit; indexed product and documentation text supplied the evidence. These limitations are retained rather than describing all resources as live-tested.

Operating systems are assigned only from explicit publisher support, installer documentation, or release evidence. A Fuse, DCTL, PowerGrade, or Lua file does not automatically receive all-platform icons. A free product may still require paid Resolve Studio. Product price, Resolve edition, and licensing are separate facts. Exact sale prices, marketplace review stars, and search crawl dates are not presented as GitHub stars or project update ages.

No purchases, downloads, installations, account registrations, or uploads were performed. Discovery used public tool/product pages; personal bookmark data and local paths are absent from the published data. This is a broad, bounded discovery pass, not a claim to have searched every website or verified every product in a marketplace.
